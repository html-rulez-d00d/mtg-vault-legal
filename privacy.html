MTG Vault Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MTG Vault mobile app (the "App"), published by Coding Buffet ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it. MTG Vault does not require an account. Your card collection lives on your device, not on our servers.

Summary

  • We do not run our own servers and we do not collect your name, email, or contact details through the App.
  • Card scanning happens on your device. Camera images are not uploaded.
  • Your collections, cards, rules and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The App uses Google services (Firebase and AdMob) for analytics, crash reporting, performance monitoring and ads. These services collect device and usage data as described below.
  • Card lookups are sent to the public Scryfall API.

1. Information stored on your device

The following data is created by you and stored locally in the App's private storage. We cannot access it.

  • Your card collections, card entries, quantities, finishes, notes and collection rules.
  • Cached card data, card images and price history downloaded from Scryfall.
  • App preferences and your monthly scan count.

If you use the backup or export feature, the App creates a file containing this data and saves or shares it wherever you choose. Once that file leaves the App, its handling is up to you and the service you send it to.

Uninstalling the App deletes this data from your device, unless you exported a backup.

2. Camera

The App asks for camera access so you can scan cards. Captured frames are processed on your device using Google ML Kit text recognition and on-device image matching. Photos are written only to temporary storage while they are processed. They are not uploaded to us or to any third party, and they are not saved to your photo library.

3. Card lookups (Scryfall)

To identify cards and fetch prices, the App sends requests to the Scryfall API (scryfall.com). These requests contain search terms such as card names, set codes and collector numbers. Like any web server, Scryfall receives your IP address and basic request information. Scryfall's handling of that data is governed by its own privacy policy. Card images and price data are provided by Scryfall and its sources.

4. Analytics, crash reporting and performance (Google Firebase)

We use Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring to understand how the App is used, to fix crashes and to improve speed. These services may collect:

  • An app instance identifier and installation identifier generated by Firebase
  • Device model, operating system version, app version, language and region settings
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city level)
  • Usage events, such as screens viewed, features used and whether you have a Pro subscription
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information about the state of the App when an error occurred
  • Performance traces, such as app start time and network request timing

We do not send your collection contents, scanned images, or any contact details to Firebase. See Firebase privacy information.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows ads served by Google AdMob, including banner ads and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for bonus features. To deliver, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device and app information, and your interactions with ads

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). Where required by law, such as in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. Learn how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Pro subscribers do not see banner ads.

6. Purchases

MTG Vault Pro subscriptions are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The store tells the App whether you have an active subscription so Pro features can be unlocked. Your purchase history is governed by the store's own privacy policy.

7. Links to other sites

The App contains links to third-party websites, such as TCGplayer for buying cards. Some of these links may be affiliate links. When you open a link, the third-party site may collect information under its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for those sites.

8. How information is used and shared

Information collected through the services above is used to operate the App, show ads in the free version, diagnose crashes, measure usage and improve features. We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers named in this policy (Google and Scryfall), which process it to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required by law.

9. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Firebase Analytics data is retained for the period configured in our Firebase project (between 2 and 14 months). Crashlytics crash data is retained for 90 days. AdMob retention is governed by Google's policies.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Camera: you can revoke camera permission at any time in your device settings. Scanning will stop working, but the rest of the App remains available.
  • Ads: you can limit ad personalization through your device settings or the consent prompt. Subscribing to Pro removes banner ads.
  • Local data: you can delete cards and collections in the App, or remove everything by uninstalling it.
  • Access and deletion: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold an account for you, most data is either on your device or held by Google under the identifiers described above. Contact us and we will help you exercise your rights, including asking Google to delete analytics data associated with your app instance.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are your consent (personalized ads) and our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the App (analytics, crash reporting, performance monitoring and Scryfall lookups).

11. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

12. Security

Data in transit to Scryfall and Google is encrypted with HTTPS. Local data is stored in the App's private storage, protected by your device's operating system. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes may also be announced in the App.

14. Contact
